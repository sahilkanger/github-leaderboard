# Code of conduct

This is a friendly project about people who write code. Keep it that way.

- Be kind and assume good intent, in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- The people on the leaderboard did not ask to be on it. Do not mock, single out, or contact anyone because of their numbers or where they live.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Disagree about code, not about people.

Problems can be raised with the maintainer through a [private report](https://github.com/wbp318/github-leaderboard/security/advisories/new) or by opening an issue. Anything that breaks these rules can be edited, hidden, or removed, and repeat offenders blocked.
